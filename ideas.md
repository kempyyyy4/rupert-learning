# Ideas inbox

Dump anything here. Nothing below Ep 2 is decided. We pick episodes based on what Ep 1 and 2 show viewers like.

## Series spine (working)
1. **Ep 1:** Rupert goes from a heap on the floor to running. Ends on a 100m time trial; the gap to 9.58 sets up Ep 2.
2. **Ep 2:** Rupert vs Usain Bolt. 100m track, Bolt's 9.58 as a ghost runner.
